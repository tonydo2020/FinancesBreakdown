About FinancesBreakdown

A personal finance channel about one question: when can a regular person afford to stop working?

The channel

FinancesBreakdown publishes long-form episodes and Shorts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Each episode builds one hypothetical person, with a real job, a realistic salary, spending and debt, and works through their finances step by step until we know when they could reach financial independence.

The goal is to make retirement math concrete. Instead of rules of thumb, you see the actual numbers for someone like you, and what changes when they pay off a card, max out a 401(k), or switch jobs.

How episodes are made

The creator writes the methodology and reviews the numbers. Scripts are generated from a financial simulation and public data, narrated with the creator's own voice using voice-cloning software, and illustrated with AI-generated artwork. All data sources are cited in each video's description. See how we calculate.

The FinancesBreakdown app

FinancesBreakdown also has a small desktop app, used only by the channel's creator, that publishes the channel's finished videos to the creator's own accounts. It is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos, titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails to the FinancesBreakdown YouTube channel (YouTube Data API).
  • Posts the same videos to the creator's own TikTok account (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API).
  • Publishes Reels to the creator's own Instagram and Facebook accounts (Meta APIs).
  • Reads the channel's own statistics to see which videos perform best (YouTube Analytics API).

Sign-in and data

The app signs in with each platform's own login page (OAuth 2.0), only for the creator's accounts. Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the creator's computer and are never sold or shared. Details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
